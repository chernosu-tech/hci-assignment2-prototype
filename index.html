TogetherStep (陪你走一步)

Interactive prototype by Graduate Team 7, CSC5056 Introduction to Human-Computer Interaction, CUHK-Shenzhen. An older adult shares one chosen app with a family helper, who points at and explains the next step; the older adult taps it herself. All calls, sharing and notifications are simulated.
